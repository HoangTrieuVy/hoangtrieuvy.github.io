POLYVERSE

Time has shattered into crystal shards. Each shard is one frozen age of civilization.

A low-poly 3D real-time strategy game. Build from a handful of settlers, raise an army, and outlast rival builders for control of the shard.

The idea

One age. No advancement. Win with what you were given.

Before each match you pick a Timestone — one of five frozen historical eras. There is no teching up mid-game: you build, fight and win using only your chosen era's technology. A Stone Age player and a Future player can meet on the same shard, and each has to make their own age work.

Timestones

Dawnstone

Stone Age

Gatherer · Brawler · Slinger

Bronzestone

Bronze Age

Villager · Spearman · Archer

Ironstone

Medieval

Peasant · Knight · Crossbowman

Gearstone

Industrial / Modern

Engineer · Rifleman · Tank

Voidstone

Future / Digital

Technician · Drone Scout · Swarm Drones

Factions

Tidecallers

Ocean and coastal. At home on water and shoreline.

Ironwardens

Mountain and mining. Built around extraction and hard ground.

Plainstriders

Plains and cavalry. Speed and open-field pressure.
